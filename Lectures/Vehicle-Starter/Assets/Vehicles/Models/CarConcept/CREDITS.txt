Changes for this course project:
- Converted from glTF to FBX.
- Body parts merged into one mesh; each wheel (tire, rim, brake disc) merged into one mesh
  with its pivot at the wheel center, and the front wheels straightened.
- Khronos and 3D Commerce logos removed from the tire sidewalls, rims and license plate.
- Materials rebuilt for Unity URP; material variants and the baked occlusion map dropped.
